# Bourbon Brothers 

App Concept: BourbonConnect

Build a mobile and web application called “BourbonConnect” that serves as a nationwide community for bourbon enthusiasts. The primary purpose of the platform is to connect bourbon lovers, collectors, retailers, and distilleries through discussion, discovery, and compliant commerce opportunities.

Core Features

Community & Social Network

User profiles with bourbon preferences, collections, wish lists, and tasting notes.

Discussion forums organized by topics such as reviews, rare finds, hunting tips, distillery news, and bourbon education.

Direct messaging between members.

Local and national bourbon groups.

Event listings for tastings, festivals, barrel picks, and distillery tours.

Bourbon Discovery

Searchable bourbon database with reviews, ratings, tasting notes, MSRP information, and release history.

User-generated reviews and rankings.

Collection tracking and bottle inventory management.

Retailer & Distillery Marketplace Connections

Directory of licensed alcohol retailers and distilleries.

Retailers can advertise available inventory, special releases, and store picks.

Users can express interest in buying, selling, or trading bottles where legally permitted.

Lead-generation system that connects users with licensed retailers who can facilitate compliant transactions.

State-by-state compliance tools and shipping eligibility information.

Business Portal

Dedicated dashboards for retailers and distilleries.

Advertising campaign management.

Sponsored listings and featured bottle promotions.

Analytics showing impressions, engagement, and customer leads.

Revenue Model

The primary revenue source should be:

Retailer advertising subscriptions.

Distillery advertising and sponsored content.

Featured listings and promoted products.

Premium business accounts.

Event promotion packages.

The platform’s focus should remain community-driven, with advertising integrated naturally and not overwhelming the user experience.

Add a direct messaging feature where members can connect to each other directly.

add a bourbon exchange that is compliant with state laws where members can sell their bourbon finds to licensed retailers. 

Design & User Experience

Premium, modern bourbon-inspired aesthetic.

Dark theme with copper, oak, and whiskey-colored accents.

Clean social-media-style feed.

Easy navigation between Community, Discover, Events, and Marketplace sections.

Highly engaging mobile-first design.

Compliance Requirements

Age verification (21+).

Compliance with federal, state, and local alcohol regulations.

No direct facilitation of unlawful alcohol sales or shipments.

All transactions involving alcohol must occur through properly licensed retailers and authorized channels where required by law.

Target Audience

Bourbon collectors

Casual bourbon enthusiasts

Whiskey clubs

Licensed liquor retailers

Distilleries

Bourbon event organizers

Mission: Create the largest digital community for bourbon enthusiasts while helping retailers and distilleries reach highly engaged consumers through advertising, education, and compliant commerce connections
